Let not your hearts be troubled. Believe in God; believe also in Me. John 14:1

The 10th of September will be the first anniversary of Charlie Kirk’s death. He was an amazing young man who loved God and this nation and was willing to take the dialogue and debate to the people: especially the university campuses.

Charlie recognized something that St. Augustine said, “He who denies the existence of God, has some reason for wishing that God did not exist.” Knowledge of the true living God requires a change, and those who do not want to change their sinful behavior would prefer to believe that God does not exist.

We honor Charlie by remembering his life. We honor God by acknowledging Him as the Creator of the heavens and earth.
