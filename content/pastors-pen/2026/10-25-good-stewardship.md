The manifold wisdom of God might be made known by the church… Ephesians 3:10

God has given us wisdom to be good stewards over this earth. The resources come from heaven; therefore, our knowledge of stewardship must grow. That steward begins with tithes and offerings, because we demonstrate our faithfulness or our disobedience here. Nevertheless, our steward ship does not stop there: we manage our gifts, our time, and our relationships – and that responsibility helps shape a better tomorrow.

God’s blessings on your like begin with your faithfulness to return to Him what He has given you. He knows how to open the windows of heaven; you do your part; He will do His.
