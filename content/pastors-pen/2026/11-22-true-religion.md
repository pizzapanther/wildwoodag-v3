Pure and undefiled religion before God and the Father is this: to visit orphans and widows in their trouble, and to keep oneself unspotted from the world. James 1:27

When we receive the newsletters from the Pleasant Hills Children’s Home and sister Kelly shares the heartbreaking stories with us, I can feel the gratefulness in the hearts of the children who now have a bed to sleep in or don’t have to worry about when they will eat again.

No one can do everything, but everyone can do something! The collective giving from each of us allows our gifts to make a difference in the lives of children. Let us recognize that we are blessed and be thankful for what God has given us.
