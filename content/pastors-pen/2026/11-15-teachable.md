At that time Jesus answered and said, “I thank You, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that You have hidden these things from the wise and prudent and have revealed them to babes. Matthew 11:25 NKJV

God’s people are instructed to grow in faith and grace. We are not to continue like infants sucking on a bottle but rather grow up and consume solid food. We are also told to have childlike faith. Bill Johnson said, “I must come to that which I understand with a childlike heart because what I know can keep me from what I need to know if I don’t remain a novice.” I would say, remain teachable! No matter how much you have grown or know, or your level of education in theology or any other field, He can reveal more to you if you are open, and I will say it again, if you are teachable.
